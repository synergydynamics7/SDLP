intersection

Synergy Dynamics is a cross-disciplinary research firm focused on applied physics, engineering systems design, and intellectual property creation. We operate at the boundaries where thermal science, materials chemistry, and electrical systems converge.

Our work is grounded in first-principles thinking — building computational tools, running validated simulations, and publishing findings that push the boundary of what is known. From finite element thermal models to CFD propulsion nozzle optimization, we pursue problems worth solving.

We are early-stage, founder-led, and deliberately small. Three disciplines. One shared conviction: rigorous science produces lasting intellectual property.
