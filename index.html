02 — Internal platform / active R&D

Flowlab.
Less fragmented.

Engineering work often breaks into too many disconnected steps. Flowlab is an attempt to connect those steps so a difficult problem can move more clearly from question to decision.

Explore the technical idea

Flowlab is a proprietary engineering workflow platform under development. It explores how problem definition, models, computation, analysis, interpretation, and design iteration can be represented as one structured computational workflow rather than a sequence of manual handoffs.

Aerothermal physics / 2025

How does a material change heat flow through a phone?

Active researchThermal study

We compared chassis and cover materials to understand how heat moves through a phone-like structure. The work combines simulation with a custom numerical check.

Source figure: Mali, “Smartphone thermal synergy” (2026), material-combination comparison.

Explore the technical work

Methods: 2D steady-state finite element analysis in ANSYS, compared across chassis and cover materials, with a custom one-dimensional explicit finite volume method in Python for cross-checking. The supplied paper includes temperature fields, material comparisons, and solver validation.

Interpretation: This is simulated work. It is presented as an engineering investigation, not as experimental validation or a commercial product result.

Aerothermal physics / 2026

Can bio-inspired nozzle surfaces make flow more uniform?

Active researchCFD study

We compared a conventional converging-diverging nozzle with a modified geometry inspired by natural flow-control structures. The question is whether changing the wall near the throat changes pressure behaviour.

Source figure: Mali, “Computational Fluid Dynamics Investigation of Bio-Inspired Flow Control Features in a Converging-Diverging Nozzle” (2026), pressure-field comparison.

Explore the technical work

Methods: OpenFOAM v13, incompressible turbulent flow, identical 50 m/s inlet velocity, and comparison of velocity and pressure distributions. The study reports pressure variation changing from 3954.48 Pa to 1979.22 Pa, approximately 49.95%, alongside an 8.26% reduction in peak velocity.

Evidence: The figures shown here are extracted from the supplied presentation of the CFD study. The paper reports no mesh-independence study, experimental validation, drag-force calculation, pumping-power evaluation, or efficiency metric.

Electrical systems / software study

Can a circuit be described clearly enough for a computer to solve?

DemonstratedPython / NumPy

A small Python circuit solver turns a component list into a solvable electrical model. It is a focused study in making circuit relationships explicit, inspectable, and computable.

Explore the technical work

Method: The script accepts a netlist of resistors and independent voltage sources, including component names, node pairs, and values. It builds the conductance matrix, augments it with voltage-source constraints using Modified Nodal Analysis, and solves the resulting linear system with NumPy.

Scope: The current solver is a compact educational and computational project. The source reviewed here documents resistor and voltage-source elements; no broader component support is claimed.
